## ZenithClipper 1.0.9

### New: ZenithClipper for Mac (beta)

- **Instant replay on Apple Silicon Macs.** It records with Apple's ScreenCaptureKit and the Mac's hardware encoder, keeps the same rolling buffer, and saves clips to Movies/ZenithClipper.
- **Game and system sound, minus the apps you exclude** (Spotify by default), plus your microphone if you turn it on.
- **Lives in the menu bar.** Save a clip with Option+C or from the menu, pause or resume, pick the clip folder, toggle the microphone and open at login.
- **The full editor is included.**
- It isn't signed by Apple, so the first time, right-click ZenithClipper in Applications and choose Open. Then allow Screen Recording in System Settings and reopen it.
- Not yet: Intel Macs, and automatic updates on Mac (Check for updates opens the download page).
